# Casepath product screenshots

These screenshots use fictional data and are sized at 1600 x 1000 pixels.

## Recommended four-slide sequence

1. `01-intake-and-case-details.png` — Guided intake turns a story into structured case details.
2. `03-evidence-grounding.png` — Every supported or conflicting point remains linked to the uploaded material.
3. `04-filing-route.png` — A versioned rules check explains whether the case appears to fit the supported filing route.
4. `05-cjts-download.png` — The user reviews every populated field and downloads a six-page CJTS entry guide.

## Additional screenshots

- `02-document-processing.png` — Extraction status, duplicates, unreadable files, and unsupported formats remain visible.
- `06-case-overview.png` — A single dashboard summarises amount, next step, open questions, and documents.
- `07-prepare-to-file-choice.png` — Prepare to file becomes available only after a current supported route check.

The screenshots are product demonstrations, not legal advice or representations
of a real court filing.
